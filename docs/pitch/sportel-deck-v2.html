Block 2 · Problem · 0:15–1:00 (≈95 words).

"Picture July in any Albanian municipality. The national performance system, SMP, asks every municipality for 52 indicators. They travel once a year: an Excel questionnaire, a Google Form, an official letter. Elbasan is one of three national good-practice models for validating these indicators. Even so, the number travels, and the rows behind it stay in each department's own file. And it matters: this autumn, 2.5 million euros in performance grants are assessed on this reporting. [Desk quote.] So the urgent question is simple: can every number prove itself, every month, not once a year?"

Block 5 · How it works · 3:00–3:30 (≈80 words). No architecture diagram.

"AI proposes, code proves. The AI does two things: it reads messy column names and proposes a mapping, and it reads a free-text question. Everything else is code: every number, every total check, removing personal data before the AI sees anything, and the label on every answer: verified, exploratory, blocked or not answerable. On our own 24 test questions it matched all 24. In the product we use the organisers' OpenAI key. Without it, Sportel still runs on rules."

Block 7 · What's next + cost · 3:55–4:35 (≈95 words).

"Four weeks of mentoring. Week one: a data agreement and three real exports. Week two: the Performance Unit validates our formulas. Week three: Sportel runs on a municipal machine. Week four: we reproduce Elbasan's 2025 return from department exports, cell by cell. That tests our tool, not the municipality. For the pilot we need the exports, one contact in the Performance Unit, and a server. Cost, as honest ranges: about 8 to 12 thousand euros to pilot-ready, 2 to 4 thousand to set up the pilot, under 100 euros a month to run, and 4 to 6 thousand a year to maintain."

Assumptions: build = 3 people × 6 weeks at a local junior-to-mid day rate; pilot setup = mappings for ~5 department exports, installation on a municipal VM, two half-day training sessions; run = a small VM (€20–50) + AI (under 1 cent per file mapping, about 2 cents per free-text question; example questions cost nothing); maintain = 2–4 days a month of updates, support and indicator changes. Who pays: municipal budget or a donor project (e.g. Bashki të Forta), with preferential pricing for Elbasan.
